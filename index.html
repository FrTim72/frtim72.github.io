Hi! I'm Yang. I work on robot learning — building generalist policies that see, understand language, and act in the physical world. Lately I've been thinking about how large-scale pretraining, video world models, and careful data curation can make vision-language-action (VLA) models more robust and sample-efficient.

I currently work with Delta-Brain on VLA training, mid-training recipes, and simulation benchmarks.

Research

My research sits at the intersection of large-scale learning and robotics. A few questions I keep coming back to:

Generalist Robot Policies

How do we turn pretrained vision-language backbones into policies that act precisely? I study action representations, mid-training data mixtures, and post-training recipes for VLA models.

World Models for Control

Video prediction models capture rich physical priors. I'm interested in using them as policy backbones, simulators, and sources of synthetic experience.

Evaluation & Benchmarks

Progress needs trustworthy measurement. I build and use simulation benchmarks (e.g. RoboTwin, RoboCasa) to evaluate policies reproducibly at scale.
